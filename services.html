01 · Skin concerns

Acne & Pimples

Acne can appear as pimples, blackheads, whiteheads or inflamed blemishes. Some people also have concerns about marks or changes in skin texture after breakouts.

The appropriate approach depends on the type and severity of the concern, personal history and a professional assessment. Avoid starting or changing treatments solely on the basis of website information.

Ask the clinic whether acne assessment and relevant treatment services are currently available.

02 · Hair concerns

Hair Transplant

People experiencing hair thinning or hair loss may want to learn about hair restoration options. The reasons for hair loss and the options worth considering can differ from person to person.

A professional assessment can help establish whether further evaluation is needed and what options may be appropriate. No particular procedure or result is guaranteed by this description.

Hair transplant is listed as an enquiry category only. Contact the clinic to confirm whether consultation or treatment is offered.

03 · Skin appearance

Scar Removal Enquiries

Scars can differ in appearance, size, depth and cause. Someone enquiring about a scar may wish to understand whether its appearance can be addressed and what options might be considered.

Appropriate options depend on the type of scar, its location, medical history and an individual assessment. Changes in appearance and treatment responses vary.

Contact the clinic to confirm current availability and whether an assessment for your concern can be arranged.

04 · Aesthetic enquiries

Tattoo Removal

People considering tattoo removal may want to ask about the available approaches, suitability and factors that influence treatment planning.

The tattoo's characteristics, skin, medical history and other individual factors can affect which options may be appropriate. Complete removal or a particular outcome should never be assumed.

Tattoo removal is an enquiry category, not a confirmation of service availability. Please contact the clinic first.

05 · Skin assessment

Mole Removal Enquiries

If you have a mole that concerns you or want to ask about its appearance, start by contacting an appropriate healthcare professional for advice on assessment.

Not every mole should be treated as a cosmetic concern. A changing, bleeding, itchy or otherwise unusual mole warrants prompt medical evaluation rather than self-treatment.

Whether removal is appropriate depends on professional assessment. Contact the clinic to ask about the services currently offered.
