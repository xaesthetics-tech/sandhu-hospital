Acne & Pimples

Acne can have different causes and may need different approaches. Enquire about an assessment for breakouts, acne marks and ongoing skin concerns.

Ask at consultation: which options suit your skin, possible side effects and how to care for your skin afterwards.

Hair Transplant

Hair restoration suitability varies with the cause of hair loss, donor hair and individual health. Request information about an assessment.

03 / SKIN

Scar Removal

Scar type, location, age and skin characteristics can influence the options that may be considered.

04 / LASER

Tattoo Removal

Tattoo removal depends on factors such as ink colours, tattoo depth, skin type and the procedure used.

Ask at consultation: available methods, possible sessions, pigment changes and aftercare.

05 / SKIN

Mole Removal

A mole should be assessed before removal is considered. Some moles may need medical examination rather than cosmetic treatment alone.

Important: seek prompt medical advice for a mole that changes in size, shape or colour, or bleeds.

Before treatment

Start with the right questions.

Every person's needs are different. Ask the clinician to explain the expected benefits, limitations, alternatives, risks, costs and follow-up before deciding on a procedure.

  • Confirm that the treatment is available at the clinic.
  • Discuss your medical history directly with the clinician.
  • Understand aftercare and when to seek help.
  • Ask for realistic expectations—not guaranteed results.
