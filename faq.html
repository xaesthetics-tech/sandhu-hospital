Helpful information

Questions, answered.

Here are some common questions about enquiries, appointments and treatment consultations at Sandhu Hospital.

How can I book an appointment?

Visit the Enquiry page and complete the form. It prepares a message in WhatsApp for you to review and send. You can also call the clinic on +91 96085 55000. Your appointment is not confirmed until the clinic confirms it.

Where is Sandhu Hospital located?

The listed address is Old Fly Over Road – Patiala Bypass Road, near Star Palace, Shamsher Nagar, Sirhind, Punjab 140406, India. Open Google Maps directions →

What are the clinic's opening hours?

Opening hours can change. Please call the clinic to confirm the current schedule and appointment availability before visiting.

Which treatments can I enquire about?

This website includes enquiry categories for acne and pimples, hair transplant, scar removal, tattoo removal and mole removal. Please contact the clinic to confirm which procedures are currently offered.

Can you tell me which treatment I need?

Website information cannot determine which treatment is appropriate for you. A qualified clinician needs to assess your concern and discuss suitable options, risks, alternatives and likely outcomes.

Are treatment results guaranteed?

No treatment outcome should be guaranteed. Results vary with the condition, procedure, individual characteristics and aftercare. Ask the clinician about realistic expectations and possible complications.

How much does a treatment cost?

Prices have not been published here because verified rates were not provided. Contact the clinic for current consultation fees, procedure costs and any additional charges before proceeding.

What should I bring to a consultation?

Ask the clinic if any specific preparation is needed. It may be useful to bring a list of medicines and relevant medical history to discuss directly with the clinician.

How can I ask a private question?

You can call the clinic or email sandhuravdeep@yahoo.co.in. Avoid sharing sensitive medical records or personal information through a public review.

How do I see patient reviews?

Use the clinic's Google Maps listing to view the latest available reviews. Reviews reflect individual experiences and do not guarantee similar outcomes.

Didn't find your answer?

Contact the clinic and ask your question directly.
